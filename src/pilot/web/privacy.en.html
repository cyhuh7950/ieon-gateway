Ieon Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-19 (first published 2026-11-14) · Applies to: the Ieon App (Android and PC), the Ieon web screen, and use by signing up to someone else's hub

1. How does Ieon work?

Ieon is an app that connects to an Ieon server you installed yourself (your PC or your hub) and lets you converse with your AI work sessions (Codex, Claude Code, etc.) linked to that server. Questions and answers are stored only on the server you connected and are not sent to the developer of the Ieon App. Ieon has no advertising or usage analytics features. If you sign up to use an Ieon hub run by someone else, that hub's operator (the hub owner) manages the sign-up information below.

Children under 14 cannot sign up to a hub run by someone else. That hub's admin cannot view members' conversation content on screen, but can technically access the server's data files.

3. Information provided to others

The developer of the Ieon App does not receive the information above, nor sell or transfer it to anyone else. The only exceptions are passing through the device maker's voice features and notification servers listed in the table above. Questions you send are delivered to the AI work sessions linked to your server (Codex, Claude Code, etc.) in accordance with those services' terms.

4. Permissions the app uses

It uses only internet (to connect to your server) and notifications. The Android app does not have microphone permission (for voice input, the device's dictation window does the recording). It does not use location, contacts, photos, or camera permissions.

5. Deletion

  • Disconnect this device: "Disconnect this device" at the bottom of the app's first screen — deletes this device's key from the server and also deletes the connection information saved in the app. You can also disconnect it in the web screen's Settings → Apps and devices → Connect Ieon App.
  • Delete conversation history: ✎ at the top of a 1:1 conversation in the app, or Connection settings on the web screen → "Delete this conversation's server history" — deletes that conversation's questions and answers remaining on the server (session history on the AI service side is managed by that service).
  • Delete your sign-up (someone else's hub): If you ask the hub owner to delete your account, your sign-up information on that hub and your Ieon records (conversation history, rooms, schedules, connected PCs, app connections) are all deleted. The original session history on your PC remains.
  • Retention period: Questions and answers are kept on the server until you delete them or the account is deleted; approval records and work folder records are deleted after 180 days. Backups created by the server admin during updates may retain data for some time after deletion. Program records (logs) do not contain questions, answers, or secrets.
  • Delete everything: Deleting your server (the Ieon program) and its data folder deletes all information. Deleting the app deletes the information on the device.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we will announce it on this page and through in-app notices.
